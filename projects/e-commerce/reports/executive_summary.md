## 👥 RFM Customer Segmentation Findings

Using Recency, Frequency, and Monetary scoring (1 to 5 scale), the customer base was categorized into 6 distinct action-oriented segments:

1. **Champions (18.2% of Revenue)**:
   - *Characteristics*: Highest recency (avg < 25 days), order frequency > 8, monetary spend > $2,500.
   - *Strategy*: Early access to new product drops, VIP loyalty program rewards, ambassador referral incentives.
2. **Loyal Customers (26.5% of Revenue)**:
   - *Characteristics*: Regular purchase cycle, high response to cross-selling campaigns.
   - *Strategy*: Personalized recommendation bundles and tiered subscription discounts.
3. **At Risk (21.4% of Revenue)**:
   - *Characteristics*: High historical monetary value, but haven't placed an order in > 90 days.
   - *Strategy*: Win-back email sequences with personalized "We Miss You" discounts (15-20% promo codes).
4. **About to Sleep / Need Attention (16.8% of Revenue)**:
   - *Characteristics*: Declining frequency, average recency ~60-90 days.
   - *Strategy*: Targeted re-engagement via SMS & Social Media retargeting ads.
5. **Lost Customers (17.1% of Revenue)**:
   - *Characteristics*: Inactive for > 180 days with single-purchase history.
   - *Strategy*: Low-cost automated email reactivation nurture campaigns.
